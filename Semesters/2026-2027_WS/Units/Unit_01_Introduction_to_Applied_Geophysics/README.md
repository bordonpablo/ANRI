# Unit 01 — Introduction to Applied Geophysics

## Overview

This unit introduces the field of applied geophysics in the context of natural resource investigation. It covers the scope of the course, the physical principles underlying geophysical prospection, the main categories of methods, and their typical applications in environmental and resource management. Students gain a broad orientation before diving into individual methods in subsequent units.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

- Describe the main categories of geophysical methods and the physical properties they measure
- Match geophysical methods to appropriate investigation targets
- Situate geophysics within multidisciplinary resource investigation workflows

---

## Key Concepts

- Physical properties of earth materials and classification of geophysical methods
- Overview of methods covered in the course (GPR, GIS, magnetometry, ERT, airborne)
- Survey design fundamentals and data interpretation approaches
