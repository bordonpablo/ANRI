# Unit 05 — Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT)

## Overview

This unit covers the principles and application of Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT), a DC electrical method used to image subsurface resistivity variations in 2D and 3D. Topics include the physics of current flow in the ground, electrode configurations, multi-electrode acquisition systems, inversion algorithms, and interpretation. Applications in groundwater, environmental, and geotechnical investigations are emphasized.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

- Explain DC resistivity principles and the concept of apparent resistivity
- Plan and execute a multi-electrode ERT survey
- Invert and interpret ERT data in geological and hydrogeological contexts

---

## Key Concepts

- Electrical resistivity of earth materials and controlling factors
- Electrode configurations and multi-electrode acquisition
- 2D inversion and data quality assessment
- Interpretation and hydrogeological/environmental applications
