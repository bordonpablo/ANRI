# Unit 04 — Magnetometry

## Overview

This unit covers the theory and practice of ground-based magnetic surveys for near-surface and shallow geological investigations. Topics include the physics of the Earth's magnetic field, instrumentation, field acquisition, data corrections, processing, and interpretation of magnetic anomalies. Applications in mineral exploration, archaeology, and environmental investigations are discussed.

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

- Describe the Earth's magnetic field and the magnetic properties of geological materials
- Design and execute a ground magnetic survey and apply standard data corrections
- Interpret magnetic anomaly data for geological and applied targets

---

## Key Concepts

- Earth's magnetic field and rock magnetic properties
- Instrumentation and field acquisition strategy
- Data corrections and processing filters
- Anomaly interpretation and applications
